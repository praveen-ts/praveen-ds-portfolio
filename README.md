# Praveen T S — Data Science & Applied AI Portfolio

Portfolio of data science and applied AI projects spanning finance, retail, insurance, and healthcare — built to demonstrate cross-industry technique transferability alongside production experience in pharma analytics.

## Projects

### [Credit Risk Scorecard](./credit-risk-scorecard)
WOE/IV-based logistic regression scorecard (AUC 0.823, Gini 0.647) outperforming XGBoost on UCI German Credit data. Fully interpretable, regulation-ready credit risk model.

### [Customer LTV & Multi-Touch Attribution](./olist-ltv-attribution)
XGBoost LTV model (R² = 0.41) and 6-model attribution comparison, including Markov chain removal-effect attribution, on 96,478 real Olist e-commerce orders.

### [CRM Retention Intelligence Agent](./crm-retention-agent)
Retention risk model (AUC = 0.76) paired with a LangGraph agent and per-customer RAG layer (ChromaDB) for natural-language retention insight queries.

### [Healthcare Patient Readmission](./healthcare-readmission)
30-day readmission prediction with a documented simple-to-complex model progression, SHAP interpretability, patient clustering, and Prophet-based admission forecasting.

### [Retail + Finance Forecasting & Risk Platform (M5 Flagship)](./m5-retail-finance-flagship)
End-to-end pipeline combining retail demand forecasting (Prophet/ARIMA/LightGBM), financial risk modeling (XGBoost + SHAP), and a RAG layer — on Walmart M5 data.

**Note on data & methodology:** every project discloses its data source (real vs. synthetic), key limitations, and the reasoning behind model/technique choices. Interpretability and analytical honesty are treated as first-class requirements throughout, not afterthoughts.
